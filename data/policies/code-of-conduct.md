# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Code of Conduct describes the standards of behaviour the Company expects from everyone who works for it. It applies to Full-Time employees, Contract employees and Interns, at every Company location and when working remotely. It also applies at work-related events and in any online conversation that involves the Company. Following the Code, and the law, is a condition of working here.

## 2. Integrity and Honesty

Employees must be honest in all their work. This means not falsifying records, timesheets, attendance, expense claims or reports, and not misleading colleagues, customers or the Company. An employee who makes a mistake should report it promptly instead of hiding it.

## 3. Respect and a Safe Workplace

Every person is to be treated with dignity and respect. Discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital status, nationality or any other personal characteristic is not tolerated, and neither is bullying, intimidation or abusive language.

**Sexual harassment.** The Company has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, in line with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. Complaints go to the Internal Committee (IC), which is set up at every location, and are investigated confidentially.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

An employee must not let personal interests, or the interests of family or friends, influence Company decisions. Examples include hiring or managing a relative, awarding business to a company the employee has an interest in, or holding a significant financial stake in a competitor, customer or supplier. Any potential conflict must be disclosed to the line manager and HR in writing.

## 5. Outside Employment

Full-Time employees must not take on any other paid work, including freelance and consulting work, without written approval from their department head and HR. Working for a competitor, or on work that uses Company time, equipment or information, is not allowed under any circumstances.

## 6. Confidentiality and Data Protection

Employees must protect confidential information, including customer data, employee records, source code, financial data and business plans. Confidential information may be shared only with people who need it for their work, and it must not be shared outside the Company without authorisation. This duty continues after employment ends. Personal data must be handled according to the Company's data protection rules and applicable law. A suspected data breach must be reported to IT Security immediately.

## 7. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, accounts and money are for business use. Limited personal use of the internet and email is allowed if it does not interfere with work or break the law. Employees must not install unlicensed software, bypass security controls or share their passwords.

## 8. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees must not ask for gifts or favours from customers, suppliers or partners. A gift or hospitality worth up to ₹2,000 may be accepted if it is occasional and does not influence a business decision. Anything above ₹2,000 must be declined or reported to HR. Cash gifts are never acceptable.

## 9. Attendance and Leave

Employees must follow the Leave Policy and the Work From Home Policy. Being absent for 3 or more consecutive working days without approval is misconduct.

## 10. Social Media and Public Statements

Employees must not speak on behalf of the Company in public unless they are authorised to. Personal posts must not disclose confidential information, or harass or defame colleagues, customers or the Company.

## 11. Substance Use

Working under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs is not permitted, and neither is possessing or selling illegal drugs on Company premises or at Company events.

## 12. Raising Concerns

Anyone who sees or suspects a breach of this Code should report it to their manager, to HR at hr@company.com, or confidentially to ethics@company.com. Sexual harassment complaints go to the Internal Committee. The Company acknowledges every report within 3 working days. A person who raises a concern in good faith is protected, and retaliation against them is itself a serious breach of this Code.

## 13. Consequences of Breaching the Code

A breach of this Code is handled fairly and after an investigation. Depending on how serious it is, the outcome can be a verbal or written warning, loss of a bonus or increment, suspension, or termination of employment. Serious cases, such as fraud, harassment or a breach of confidentiality, can lead to immediate termination and legal action.

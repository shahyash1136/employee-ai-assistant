# Work From Home Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Work From Home (WFH) Policy sets out when and how employees may work remotely. The Company follows a hybrid model that combines time in the office with time working from home. This policy applies to all employees at the Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Pune offices. Eligibility depends on employment type (Full-Time, Contract or Intern) and on the employee's stage of service.

## 2. The Hybrid Model

Eligible Full-Time employees work from the office at least 3 days per week and may work from home up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are team anchor days when every employee in a team is expected to be in the office, unless their manager has approved an exception. The other in-office days are agreed with the manager.

## 3. Eligibility

| Category | WFH allowance |
|---|---|
| Full-Time, confirmed (past 6-month probation) | Up to 2 days per week |
| Full-Time, on probation (first 6 months) | Up to 1 day per week, with manager approval |
| Contract employees | Up to 1 day per week, with manager approval |
| Interns | Not eligible for regular WFH. Ad hoc WFH of up to 2 days per month only with manager approval |

Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are not eligible for WFH while the plan is active.

## 4. Roles With Fixed Onsite or Shift Requirements

Some roles cannot be done remotely. The Support and Operations teams work to a shift roster set by their team lead, and WFH for these teams is decided by the team lead according to coverage needs. Employees in IT who are on infrastructure duty are required in the office during their duty days. If a role has onsite requirements, the manager must tell the employee this in writing.

## 5. Working Hours and Availability

The standard working day is 09:00 to 18:00, which is 9 hours including a 1-hour break. This applies on both office days and WFH days. During core hours, 11:00 to 16:00, employees must be reachable and must respond to messages and calls within 30 minutes. Cameras must be on for client calls and for team meetings that the organiser marks as camera-required.

## 6. Requesting WFH

A WFH day must be requested through the HR portal at least 1 working day in advance and approved by the line manager. For urgent same-day situations, the employee must message their manager before 09:30 and then submit the request in the portal the same day. Approved WFH days are recorded in the attendance system with the status WFH.

## 7. Attendance Recording

A day is recorded as Present or WFH when the employee works at least 8 hours. A day with 4 hours or more but fewer than 8 hours is recorded as Half-Day. A day with fewer than 4 hours is recorded as Absent unless leave has been approved. Employees working from home are responsible for making sure their hours are logged correctly.

## 8. Being Unwell While Working From Home

An employee who is too unwell to work must take Sick Leave under the Leave Policy. WFH is not a substitute for Sick Leave. An employee should not log a WFH day and then work reduced hours because of illness.

## 9. Extended WFH and Working From Another Location

Working from home for more than 5 consecutive working days, or working from a city other than the employee's base location, needs approval from both the department head and HR. Extended WFH is limited to 30 days per year in total, and it is approved only for reasons such as a family situation, a medical need or a relocation in progress.

Employees must work from within India. Working from outside India is not permitted, even for a short time, without written approval from HR and the Legal team, because it can create tax, legal and data-protection obligations for the Company.

## 10. Equipment and Allowances

The Company provides each Full-Time employee with a laptop and the software required for their role. Full-Time employees who are eligible for WFH receive an internet allowance of ₹1,000 per month, paid with their salary without a separate claim. Other home-office costs, such as furniture, are not reimbursed unless HR has approved them in advance. Company equipment must be used only for Company work and returned when employment ends.

## 11. Information Security While Working Remotely

- Connect to the Company VPN whenever accessing internal systems.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for Company work unless the VPN is on.
- Lock the screen whenever stepping away from the device.
- Do not let family members or other people use Company devices or see confidential information on screen.
- Do not print confidential documents at home.
- Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 2 hours.

## 12. Withdrawal of WFH Privileges

A manager may withdraw or limit WFH for an employee for repeated unavailability during core hours, missing team anchor days, poor performance or business need. The manager must inform the employee in writing and tell HR. The employee can ask HR to review the decision.

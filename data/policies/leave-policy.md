# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Leave Policy explains how much leave employees of the Company are entitled to, how to apply for it, and how it is approved and recorded. It applies to employees at all Company locations (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Pune). Entitlements differ by employment type: Full-Time employees, Contract employees and Interns. Where this policy does not give a benefit to a category, that category is not eligible for it.

## 2. Leave Year

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. All leave balances are credited and calculated on this cycle. Employees who join during the leave year receive a pro-rated entitlement for the months remaining, counted from the date of joining.

## 3. Types of Leave and Entitlement

| Leave type | Full-Time | Contract | Intern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 days per year | 4 days per year | Not applicable |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 10 days per year | 6 days per year | Not applicable |
| Earned Leave (EL) | 18 days per year | Not eligible | Not eligible |
| Intern leave | Not applicable | Not applicable | 1 day per month |

Maternity, paternity, bereavement, marriage and compensatory leave are described in the sections below. Any leave beyond the entitlement in this table is treated as Loss of Pay (LOP).

## 4. Casual Leave (CL)

Casual Leave is for short, personal or unplanned reasons such as urgent errands or family commitments. A Full-Time employee gets 8 days of Casual Leave per year and a Contract employee gets 4 days. Casual Leave cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive working days at a time. Unused Casual Leave lapses on 31 March and is not carried forward or encashed.

## 5. Sick Leave (SL)

Sick Leave is for the employee's own illness or injury, or medical appointments. A Full-Time employee gets 10 days of Sick Leave per year and a Contract employee gets 6 days. An employee who is unwell must inform their manager by 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required when Sick Leave covers more than 2 consecutive working days. Unused Sick Leave lapses on 31 March and is not carried forward or encashed.

## 6. Earned Leave (EL)

Earned Leave is for planned time off such as vacations. Only Full-Time employees earn Earned Leave. It accrues at 1.5 days for each completed month of service, giving 18 days per year. Employees in their first 6 months (the probation period) accrue Earned Leave but can use it only after they are confirmed.

Unused Earned Leave carries forward to the next leave year, up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Any balance above 30 days lapses on 31 March. Earned Leave is not encashed during employment. Accrued Earned Leave is paid out when an employee leaves the Company, subject to the maximum balance of 30 days.

Earned Leave cannot be used during the notice period without written approval from HR.

## 7. Maternity, Paternity, Bereavement and Marriage Leave

**Maternity Leave.** A Full-Time employee who is expecting a child is entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for a third or later child, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

**Paternity Leave.** A Full-Time employee who becomes a father is entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

**Bereavement Leave.** A Full-Time or Contract employee is entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or spouse's parent).

**Marriage Leave.** A Full-Time employee is entitled to 5 days of paid leave once during their employment for their own marriage. It must be taken within 3 months of the wedding date.

## 8. Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)

An employee who is asked by their manager to work on a weekend or a public holiday earns one Compensatory Off day per full day worked, or half a day for a half-day worked. Comp-off needs the manager's written approval before the work is done. It must be used within 60 days of the day it was earned, or it lapses. Comp-off is not encashed.

## 9. How to Apply for Leave

Leave must be applied for through the HR portal and approved by the line manager before the leave starts, except for Sick Leave and emergencies, which can be applied for on the first day back.

Minimum advance notice:

- Casual Leave of 1 to 2 days: 1 working day before.
- Earned Leave of 3 to 10 working days: 7 calendar days before.
- Earned Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days: 30 calendar days before.
- Maternity and paternity leave: at least 30 days before where possible.

A half-day of leave counts as 0.5 day and is recorded as Half-Day in the attendance record.

## 10. Approval Process

The line manager approves or rejects a leave request within 2 working days. If the manager has not responded after 3 working days, the request escalates to the department head. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days needs approval from both the line manager and the department head, and HR is informed. Managers can decline leave for business-critical periods but must give the employee a reason and suggest alternative dates.

## 11. Weekends and Holidays Falling Within Leave

For Earned Leave, weekends and public holidays that fall within a continuous leave period are not deducted from the leave balance.

For Casual Leave and Sick Leave, the sandwich rule applies. If an employee takes Casual or Sick Leave on the working day before and the working day after a weekend or public holiday, the weekend or holiday days in between are also counted as leave.

## 12. Public Holidays

The Company observes 12 public holidays per year. HR publishes the holiday list for each location (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Pune) in January, because some holidays differ by region. Employees may also choose 2 optional holidays per year from a published list. Days recorded as Holiday in the attendance system are not deducted from any leave balance.

## 13. Unapproved Absence

An absence without an approved leave request is recorded as Absent and treated as Loss of Pay. An unapproved absence of 3 or more consecutive working days is treated as misconduct and handled under the Code of Conduct.

## 14. Leave During Probation

An employee in their first 6 months can use Casual Leave and Sick Leave on a pro-rated basis. Earned Leave accrues during probation but can be used only after confirmation. Maternity and paternity leave are available from the date of joining for Full-Time employees.

## 15. Questions

For questions about leave balances or entitlements, contact HR at hr@company.com.

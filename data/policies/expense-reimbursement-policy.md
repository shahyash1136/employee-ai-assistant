# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Expense Reimbursement Policy explains which business expenses the Company reimburses, the limits that apply, and how to claim. It applies to Full-Time employees. Contract employees are reimbursed according to their contract terms, and Interns are reimbursed only for business travel that was approved in advance. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR).

## 2. General Principles

An expense is reimbursable only if it is a genuine business expense, is reasonable, is supported by the documents required in this policy, and has the approvals required. Employees should spend the Company's money as they would their own. Personal expenses are never reimbursable, even if they were paid on a Company trip.

## 3. Submitting a Claim

Claims are submitted through the expense portal, with receipts attached.

- Submit within 30 days of the date of the expense.
- A claim submitted after 30 days but within 60 days needs approval from the Finance Head.
- A claim submitted more than 60 days after the expense is not reimbursed.
- A receipt or invoice is required for every expense of ₹500 or more.
- For expenses under ₹500, a receipt is not needed, but the total of such receipt-free expenses cannot exceed ₹2,000 per month, and the employee must add a written note of the purpose.

## 4. Approval Matrix

Every claim needs approval based on its total amount:

| Claim amount | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹5,000 | Line manager |
| ₹5,001 to ₹25,000 | Line manager and department head |
| ₹25,001 to ₹100,000 | Department head and Finance Head |
| Above ₹100,000 | Finance Head and CFO, and pre-approval before the expense is incurred |

Employees cannot approve their own claims. Splitting one expense into several smaller claims to avoid a higher approval level is not permitted.

## 5. Processing Time and Payment

Finance reviews an approved claim within 5 working days. Claims fully approved by the 20th of the month are paid with that month's salary on the last working day of the month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid in the following month.

## 6. Travel

**Booking.** Business travel must be booked through the Company travel desk at least 7 days in advance where possible.

**Air travel.** Domestic flights are economy class for all employees. International travel needs pre-approval from the Finance Head.

**Train travel.** Managers, Leads and Architects may travel by AC 2-tier. Software Engineers and Analysts travel by AC 3-tier.

**Local travel.** Cab or auto fares for business travel are reimbursed at actual cost with a receipt. Use of a personal vehicle for business is reimbursed at ₹8 per km for a two-wheeler and ₹15 per km for a car. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

## 7. Hotel and Meals During Business Travel

Hotel cost per night is capped by designation and by city.

| Designation | Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad | Other cities |
|---|---|---|
| Software Engineer, Analyst | ₹6,000 | ₹4,500 |
| Lead, Architect, Manager | ₹9,000 | ₹6,500 |

Meals during business travel are reimbursed through a daily allowance: ₹1,200 per day for Software Engineers and Analysts, and ₹1,800 per day for Leads, Architects and Managers. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 8. Client Entertainment

Client meals and hospitality need approval from the department head before the event. The limit is ₹2,500 per person. The claim must list the names and companies of all attendees and the business purpose. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 9. Advances

An employee travelling on approved business travel can request a travel advance of up to 75% of the estimated cost. The advance must be settled by submitting the actual expense claim within 7 days after returning. Any unsettled advance is recovered from the employee's next salary.

## 10. Learning and Certification

A Full-Time employee can claim up to ₹40,000 per leave year for courses and certifications related to their role, if the manager approves in advance. If the employee leaves the Company within 12 months of the reimbursement, a pro-rated share of the amount must be repaid. Interns and Contract employees are not eligible for the learning allowance.

## 11. Internet Allowance

Full-Time employees who are eligible for work from home receive an internet allowance of ₹1,000 per month. It is paid with salary and does not need to be claimed. It is described in the Work From Home Policy.

## 12. Expenses That Are Not Reimbursable

- Fines, penalties and traffic violations.
- Alcohol and tobacco.
- Personal items and personal entertainment.
- Travel for family members.
- Gifts above ₹2,000 in value.
- Expenses with no receipt above the limit in Section 3.
- Expenses already paid for by the Company or a client.

## 13. Foreign Currency

An expense paid in foreign currency is converted to INR at the rate shown on the employee's card statement, or otherwise at the RBI reference rate on the date of the expense.

## 14. False or Misleading Claims

Submitting a false, inflated or duplicate claim is a serious breach of the Code of Conduct. Finance may audit any claim. The employee must repay the amount, and disciplinary action may follow, up to termination of employment.

## 15. Questions

For questions about this policy or a specific claim, contact Finance at finance@company.com.
